PC VR streaming at 72–207 Hz. Your headset's full rate, not a cap.

VLXR — ViewLab XR — is a PC VR runtime and streamer for Meta Quest 3. It drives your VR games at any refresh rate the headset supports, encodes with the hardware encoder, and gives you live control from a Streamer app and an in-headset menu.

The headline

Every refresh rate the headset supports

Quest 3 panels top out at 144 Hz through most PC VR stacks. VLXR streams at whatever the headset is set to — 72 through 207 Hz, with 240 experimental — and paces frames to hit it, measured second by second.

72–207 Hz, selectable

Pick the rate in the Streamer app or double-tap the menu button in-headset — the stream re-rates without relaunching the game.

Precise frame pacing

One-frame buffering and per-second stats keep frame times inside the budget for the chosen rate — the difference between smooth and almost-smooth at 200 Hz.

Honest numbers

Per-second stats.csv and live readouts for fps, latency, encode, network and decode — measured on real hardware, published with the caveats.

Honest status

Early, measured, one setup

VLXR is early software tested on a single configuration — RX 9070 XT, Quest 3 on a wired LAN adapter. Measured results are published with their conditions rather than benchmarks marketing can't support. It needs an a capable GPU (hardware encoder), Windows 10/11, a Quest 3 in developer mode and a solid local network.

Measured

180 Hz held 180 fps headset-side; 207 Hz held 207 fps every second with ~25–30 ms total latency on the test rig.

Requirements

a capable GPU, Windows 10/11, Meta Quest 3 developer mode with the VLXR client, Wi-Fi 6/6E or USB-C Ethernet on the headset.
