The stream bench

Every refresh rate has a frame-time budget — 6.9 ms at 144 Hz, 4.8 ms at 207 Hz. Codec, resolution and buffering decide whether you hold it. This bench models that trade-off the way VLXR's streamer exposes it: drag the controls and watch the pacing graph. Illustration, not a screenshot.

What it does

Built for the frame budget

VLXR exists because high refresh rates live or die on pacing — a dropped frame at 200 Hz is felt twice as hard as at 100. It's tuned end to end for that, and controlled where you actually need it: on the PC, and inside the headset without taking it off.

Every rate, held

72 through 207 Hz, selectable live — the stream re-rates without relaunching the game.

Control mid-game

Codec, refresh rate, bitrate and buffering adjust on a running stream, from the PC or an in-headset menu.

Honest telemetry

fps, latency, encode, network and decode — measured per second and shown where you can see it.
